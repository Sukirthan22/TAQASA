The leakage rail

Why the comparison means anything

Every invoice carries a hidden latent_cause, and no policy ever receives it. The harness hands policies an ObservedState built by redact() — only what a real collections team would know on the morning of day N: what it sent, when, and what came back. Reading ground truth from a policy requires visibly going around a named function.

The world model rolls no dice. Every random draw a customer will ever need is made once at generation time and frozen onto the invoice, so the baseline can be run, time rewound, and the agent run over the same invoice. The difference is attributable to the policy alone.
